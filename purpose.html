Coding Skills

Coding skills are a big part of our club because we enjoy using the vast power of programming to create things that can help people in our community in a variety of ways. As we work on our projects to help others, we also collectively help each other build coding skills that we can use in our careers and personal applications.

Design Skills

Design skills are essential to our club because we like to make things look just as appealing and organized as they are functional, allowing more people to enjoy and easily use the creations we develop. We can also apply the design skills that we build to our plans for the future, whether related to work, personal matters, or otherwise.

Reducing Pollution

We work to combat the effects of pollution by designing projects that can raise our community's awareness about pollution and its impacts in addition to finding solutions to physically reduce pollution in our area.
