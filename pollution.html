Primary Pollution Problems

  • Pollution can harm nearby wildlife, as animals that try to interact with trash that they cannot identify may accidentally consume harmful materials or injure themselves otherwise.

  • Pollution can disturb local ecosystems, as water can carry dangerous chemicals from trash to a pond or other body of water and harm the creatures that interact with it.

  • Pollution continues to fill up landfills, creating a need for more landfills to be constructed every year.

  • Pollution is often thrown into our oceans, which leads to marine animals harming themselves with all kinds of trash and large trash islands forming in the water.

  • Pollution in high amounts often overwhelms recycling centers, forcing them to put trash into landfills and the ocean anyway.



Fighting Pollution

  • Pick up trash in your area; keep your part of the world clean.

  • Raise awareness about pollution in your community; inform the people around you.

  • Repurpose any trash you can; an old tin of mints can hold toothpicks, screws, and more!

  • Use reusable items when you can; a metal water bottle can last far longer than many plastic water bottles ever could.

  • When you throw things away, try to put recyclable materials aside so you can take them to a local recycling center later.
