Primary Pollution Problems

  • Pollution can harm nearby wildlife, as animals who try to interact with trash that they cannot identify may accidentally consume harmful materials or injure themselves otherwise.

  • Pollution can disturb local ecosystems, as water can carry dangerous chemicals from trash to a pond or other body of water and harm the creatures that interact with it.

  • Pollution continues to fill up landfills, creating a need for more landfills to be constructed every year.

  • Pollution is often thrown into our oceans, which leads to sea life harming themselves with all kinds of trash and large trash islands forming in the water.

  • Pollution in high amounts often overwhelms recycling centers, forcing them to put trash into landfills and the ocean anyway.
