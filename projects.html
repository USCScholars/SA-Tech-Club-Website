Club Website Project (Aug-Sept 2026)

To start us off and truly display what our club is about, we have developed this website in Visual Studio Code and are hosting it using GitHub Pages. We are also using this website as an opportunity to provide our community with a free educational resource about pollution.




Game-Making Competition (Oct 2026)

As we compete to see who can create the best game in MakeCode Arcade, we hope to be able to share our games to help various people with their mental health. The game that receives the most votes will be displayed on the home page.




Tutoring Website Project (Nov-Dec 2026)

As a step up from our club website, we hope to develop a tutoring website that helps Scholars Academy students connect with tutors to foster easy and helpful learning experiences.




Robot Project (Jan-TBD 2027)

In a large project, we plan to design and potentially build a robot that can clean up trash on land as an effort to help combat pollution and its impacts.




E-Waste Drive (April 2027)

To celebrate National Recycling Month, collect hardware that we can repurpose for our learning, and generally give electronic waste another life, we plan to collect e-waste from people who have things to donate.
